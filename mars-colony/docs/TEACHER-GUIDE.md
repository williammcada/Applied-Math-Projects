# Mars Colony v0.1.0 — teacher guide

A WILLIAM MCADA PRODUCT

## Three lessons

1. Briefing, demand, stock/rate units, conversions, ceiling module counts, habitat area. Alternate Planner/Checker.
2. Cargo and credit manifest; selected resource equation, table and graph. Explain capped storage and signed reserves.
3. Percent reduction, signed energy balance, battery tracking, reference-plan comparison, independent transfer, printed mission report/patch. Revisit earlier stages for revision; stale responses remain visible.

Times are estimates, not classroom-tested results. Allow calculators. Help is unpenalized. There is no timer, numeric grade or mastery claim. Incorrect mathematics blocks progression; mathematically correct weak designs reach Deployment delayed. Review arguments and independent participation yourself.

## Reference mathematics

Demand per day: water 72 L, oxygen 19.2 kg, food 14.4 kg. Ten days:720 L,192 kg,144 kg. 0.8 kg=800 g;3 L=3000 mL. L/kg/kWh are stocks; corresponding per-day units are rates. Minimum counts:3 water,3 oxygen,3 habitats; capacities90 L/day,24 kg/day,24 people. Area72 m² and3 m²/person.

Baseline 3W/3O/3H/4S/1B, standard crate:25,300 cr,3,185.2 kg; remaining700 cr/214.8 kg. Water W(t)=144+18t; oxygen O(t)=38.4+4.8t; food F(t)=172.8−14.4t before caps. Day0/5/10:water144/234/324 L;oxygen38.4/62.4/86.4 kg;food172.8/100.8/28.8 kg. Constant net increase does not continue above tank capacity.

Load114 kWh/day. Normal production144; storm per-array27, total108, balance−6. Percentage remaining75%. Battery days3,4,5,6,7:36,30,24,18,36 kWh. Unserved storm energy0. Battery is storage, not production. Daily model does not prove continuous operation overnight.

Reference B (5 arrays/no battery) costs25,500 cr, cargo3,205.2 kg:200 cr/20 kg more than A;21 kWh/day storm surplus. Both reference designs are resilient. Lean baseline costs25,100 cr, cargo3,170.8 kg and ends with14.4 kg food:Mission ready. Two habitats or inadequate solar/storage delays deployment. Over-budget designs can still be calculated and reported.

Transfer A:30 settlers →90 L/day →3 water modules. Transfer B:28 →84 L/day →3 modules. Have the other partner look away. Submitted responses are covered in student view but printed for teacher review; the application cannot establish independent participation.

## Artifacts and operation

Print mission report/patch after all checkpoints, including evidence table, cargo manifest, timeline, graph, comparison and attempt history. A4/Letter browser print is the intended route. Use Export progress or Export all before device changes/deletion. Imports are copies; wrong project/schema or corrupt files are rejected. Same-schema patches remain compatible; there is no older Mars schema to migrate.

Storage is local, alias-only, with no accounts, telemetry, remote submission or live shared session. Standalone desktop HTML runs offline. Hosted reload after closing the browser still requires network; no service worker is promised. School-network, physical-iPad and printer acceptance must be tested in school.
