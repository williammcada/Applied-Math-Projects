# Food Truck v0.1.0 — teacher guide

**A WILLIAM MCADA PRODUCT · William McAda**

Advanced Grade 5 pre-algebra / Saxon 8/7. Three proposed 45-minute lessons; pairs share a device and alternate Planner/Checker. Times are estimates, not classroom trial results. This is a mathematical business simulation, with no cooking or food handling.

## Before the lesson

Open the hosted page on the actual school iPad/network, or the standalone HTML in a desktop browser. Open Teacher → Run diagnostics, test export/import, and inspect report printing. Use aliases. Each browser/location has its own saved work; move work with a JSON export. File-preview applications on iPad are not the supported delivery method.

All prices and demand forecasts are authored classroom assumptions. The three cuisine identities share one explicit costing model; they are not three real recipes. No random customer roll changes the results.

## Lesson sequence

1. **Invitation, recipe, ledger:** name the truck, choose its identity and provisional stock. Scale every ingredient and package. Separate variable purchases from fixed launch fees. After Purchasing ledger, export and swap roles.
2. **Pricing and investor pitch:** read the demand table, calculate contribution/markup, build revenue/expense/profit functions, solve exact break-even versus first positive profit, enter tables and ordered pairs. After Investor pitch, export and swap roles.
3. **Stock/service, launch, comparison:** apply all three sales limits, account for all prepared stock, and freeze Plan A. Create a distinct Plan B, recheck changed work, and recommend either. Complete separate transfer responses. Print the report/menu and export progress.

The role-swap prompts occur once per lesson boundary, not each time a revision revisits a screen. Named stages remain revisitable. A wrong answer blocks the affected progression; Back, Help, Save, export and draft printing remain available. Correct negative profit is accepted.

## Mathematics and answer reference

One 20-portion batch: 2 kg base at $5/kg; 3 kg vegetables at $6/kg; 2 kg protein at $20/kg; 0.5 L sauce at $20/L; 20 packages at $0.60. Line costs $10/$18/$40/$10/$12 total **$90**, or **$4.50/portion**. Fixed fees: $200 stall + $150 hire + $100 setup = **$450**.

At 100 portions: 10 kg base, 15 kg vegetables, 10 kg protein, 2.5 L sauce, 100 packages. Prepared variable costs: $50/$90/$200/$50/$60 = $450. Prepared variable cost and fixed expense happen to match here; they are separate quantities.

| Price | Demand | Contribution | Markup | Exact break-even | First non-loss / positive profit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $9 | 120 | $4.50 | 100.0% | 100 | 100 / 101 |
| $12 | 90 | $7.50 | 166.7% | 60 | 60 / 61 |
| $15 | 60 | $10.50 | 233.3% | 300/7 | 43 / 43 |

Sold-all functions: R(x)=px; C(x)=450+4.5x; P(x)=(p−4.5)x−450. Both coefficients must be correct; a rule that happens to produce the right value at one point is insufficient. At $12, R(0,60,100)=0,720,1200 and C=450,720,900. The crossing is (60,720). At $15 it is (300/7,4500/7); exact fractions are accepted. Whole sales are discrete even though the lines locate a continuous crossing.

Service capacity is 32×3=96. Actual sales are min(stock,demand,96). Revenue uses sales; expense is450+4.5×prepared stock. At price12/stock100, sales90, revenue1080, expense900, profit180, waste10, sell-through90%. P(90)=225 overstates profit by45 because it omits unsold-stock costs.

Top-ending plans: price12/stock80 → profit150, waste0; price12/stock100 → profit180, waste10%; price15/stock60 → profit180, waste0. The $9 sold-all break-even is unattainable at this festival's capacity, but the mathematical model is still correct.

Plan A is preserved. Plan B must change price or stock. Price12/stock80 compared with baseline gives profit change−30 and waste change−10; price15/stock60 gives profit change0 and waste change−10. Either recommendation is valid when supported; no hidden optimum is required.

Individual transfer A: P(x)=9x−360, break-even40, first positive41. B: revenue560, expense480, profit80, waste14, sell-through80%, expense based on prepared portions. Separate printable slips omit these answers.

## Input and support

Fractions/decimals and equivalent linear expressions are accepted. Example: 7.5x−450 and12x−(450+4.5x). Expressions use x, numbers, parentheses and arithmetic, not a general algebra system. Do not enter powers or other variables. Counts must be exact integer-valued numbers. Markup, waste and sell-through percentages use nearest0.1%, half-up. A total USD field does not accept USD/portion.

Calculator support is labeled on arithmetic fields. Rules, graph construction, classification and interpretation remain student work. Help uses different numbers and has no penalty. Ordered-pair fields provide keyboard/touch graph entry without dragging. Verified lines appear after the table/graph checkpoint.

## Evidence, review and endings

Festival success requires current evidence, profit≥150 and waste≤10%. Nonnegative profit missing a target is Viable first launch; a loss is Business rethink. Missing/stale evidence produces Draft. An active teacher bypass produces Provisional. Final correctness after unlimited revision is not independent mastery.

Teacher mode can record a short reasoning review or a paper/oral transfer submission. Merely selecting paper/oral does not submit work. Enter a reviewer alias and observation, then mark pending, reviewed or revise. Free prose is never semantically graded by the app. A bypass requires a reason and remains visible. A successful recheck can retire an active bypass without deleting its history.

Plan B carries unchanged checked evidence from Plan A with its origin labeled. Changed work becomes Needs recheck, retaining prior responses and contexts. Imported history is labeled and does not certify independent work. Local teacher controls cannot securely conceal answers or make records tamper-proof.

## Saved work and printing

Saved work offers New, Resume, Duplicate, Import, selected backup, Delete and Clear all Food Truck sessions. A complete session includes both plans, reviews and history. Deletion names its scope, asks confirmation, provides Cancel and offers backup. Recovery needs the exported file; previously downloaded files and other projects are untouched.

An import previews the whole file. Import as new copies is the default; named replacement offers backup first. Unsupported or malformed files leave current work unchanged. If device storage fails, keep the tab open and export. If another tab saved newer work, Reload or Save as copy resolves the conflict.

Print previews are separate: four-page student report, two-page teacher reference, one-page menu/display card, and two individual transfer slips. Choose A4 or Letter, portrait,100%, browser headers/footers off. Draft printing is allowed and labeled. Test physical grayscale output at school; generated PDFs are not a physical-printer acceptance result.
