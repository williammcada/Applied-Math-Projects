**Target 40 minutes; reuse the Science model and evidence.** Prerequisites: scientific notation, signed exponent rules, all four operations, metric conversion, ratio direction and ruler reading.

Final normalized coefficient, exponent and unit are required. Optional method entries are checked separately and preserved in attempt history. An unsubmitted method is not evidence of a correct method. Both independent exits remain distinct; paper/oral responses need recorded receipt and teacher review.

Observed scale uses the actual Science measurements, converted to mm, divided by supplied actual length. Fractions and equivalent model:actual ratios are accepted. Cell 159 mm gives 19,875 and deviation −1 mm. Earth 20 mm gives 1/640,000,000. Moon 5.5 mm gives 11/7,000,000,000. Centers 598 mm give 299/192,000,000,000 and deviation −2 mm.

The intended Moon diameter is 5.46875 mm, rounded to 5.5 mm for construction. Do not require identical inferred ratios after rounding and measurement. Compare each dimension with its construction target/tolerance: cell 160±2, Earth 20±1, Moon 5.5±1, centers 600±5 mm, endpoints included. Physical tolerance does not loosen exact academic arithmetic.
