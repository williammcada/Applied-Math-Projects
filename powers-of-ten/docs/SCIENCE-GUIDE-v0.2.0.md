# Science Day — Powers of Ten v0.2.0

**40 minutes; one pair, one model, one saved Science Evidence Passport.**

Purpose: distinguish supplied references from observations, construct a scientific representation, measure honestly, and explain a preserved and an omitted feature. No scale-factor derivation or scientific-notation checks are required today.

Prepare before the clock starts: labeled pair materials, millimeter rulers, card/paper, tape, pencils, prepared discs or reusable material, and 600 mm workspaces for cosmic pairs. Supply a teacher example of the other track. Preprint a blank passport per pair. Check template calibration (50 mm ±0.5 mm). Test the website and chosen file-transfer route on classroom iPads.

| Time | Directions | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | Museum brief, supplied source vs model, prediction | Property and prediction |
| 4–8 | Confirm track, aliases, model label and source | Identified model; Builder/Verifier |
| 8–22 | Build; swap practical roles at minute 15 | One physical model |
| 22–29 | Measure honestly; tool/resolution; peer check if available | Actual observations |
| 29–34 | Explain preserved/omitted feature; inspect other example | Short science claims |
| 34–40 | Label, pack, passport, save, download and test saved copy | Collected model + usable passport/file |

Cell: supplied exemplar diameter 8 µm; target model diameter 160 mm. Depict a biconcave disc with an intact center, no hole or nucleus. Flat paper omits 3D form. Thickness has no assigned scale. This is reference-based modeling, not observation of a living cell.

Earth–Moon: Earth 20 mm, Moon 5.5 mm, centers 600 mm apart. Use prepared components. Measure center to center, not edge gap. The representation omits motion and the complete 3D system. Mathematics will explain the rounded Moon target.

Ask “Which numbers were supplied? Which did you observe?” Record disagreement or “not checked” for the peer check; do not invent approval. Rough and out-of-tolerance models can supply complete, usable evidence.

Use a scribe, teacher-transcribed oral claims, or prepared components as needed. Omit decoration if time runs short. Begin packing at minute 34. If the evidence is incomplete, export an honest draft and record that the 40-minute goal was not met. A teacher example used next class must have its own labeled provenance.

Finish Science Day lists missing evidence together. A download click is not proof of storage: **Test saved copy** reopens the file and compares session, revision and Science fields without replacing active work. Print/save the one-page passport or copy onto the preprinted sheet. Collect passports and labeled models together.

Science completeness, copy verification, physical fit, mathematics and human review remain separate. The 40-minute classroom requirement has not yet been verified with representative students.
