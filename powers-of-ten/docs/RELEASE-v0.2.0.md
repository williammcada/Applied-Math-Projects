# Powers of Ten v0.2.0

The previous sequence mixed investigation, calculation and construction across two general lessons. This release explicitly separates a 40-minute Science lesson from a subsequent Mathematics lesson.

Science ends with one measured model, a one-page Science Evidence Passport and portable JSON. Mathematics resumes that evidence to calculate intended and observed scale and signed deviations. Download verification, paper recovery, legacy-copy migration and separate review states support the class handoff. All eight operations and the original twelve objectives remain; repeated method grids are optional, separately checked evidence.

Includes subject-specific teacher guides, a coordination sheet, printable outputs and standalone offline HTML. Primary classroom host: https://mcada-applied-math.netlify.app/powers-of-ten/ . GitHub is canonical.

See QA-REPORT-v0.2.0.md for automated checks and explicit physical-device/classroom limitations. No accounts, backend, telemetry or cloud synchronization were added.
