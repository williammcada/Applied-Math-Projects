## 1. Purpose, audience and detailed scope

- Five bespoke independent self-contained HTML applications: Road Trip Planner, Food Truck/Business Launch, Theme Park Designer, Mars Colony, Powers of Ten/Scale of Reality. Not a generator, cartridge engine, live multiplayer system or TestForge replacement.
- Advanced Grade 5 Introduction to Pre-Algebra/Saxon 8/7 context. Dossier defaults: pairs sharing iPad, desktop keyboard/mouse also supported, alternating Planner/Checker, separate individual transfer response. Targets are not classroom-test results.
- Per-project durations are proposed 45-minute lessons: 3/3/4/3/2 respectively. ELL-friendly English, metric core units, field-specific calculator support, unlimited revisions and unpenalized help; no countdown/speed score.
- Each standalone app embeds assets, runs offline and can be hosted on GitHub Pages without Netlify/Cloudflare runtime. No music/audio requirement is stronger here: dossier explicitly says no music, audio files, autoplay or sound-dependent information.
- Narrative introduction, pictures, progress, substantive field-level keyboard/touch ? help, meaningful decisions, revision and performance-responsive epilogue are required.
- Every checkpoint specifies objective, action, representation, accepted format, evaluator, units/precision, prerequisites, help/errors and evidence. Invalid academic work blocks affected progression while Save/Back/Help remain; valid weak designs reach analysis/revision rather than false math errors.
- Preserve exact/rational money evaluation, equivalent forms, explicit rounding, unit checking, dependency invalidation, first-attempt versus corrected evidence, teacher-reviewed prose/research/model inspection.
- Save/export/import and printable artifacts are core. Alias-only local data; no telemetry, automatic email/cloud submission or implied secure teacher authentication.
- Per-project delivery: versioned HTML plus byte-identical deployable index.html, teacher guide/answers, release notes, QA report. Do not deliver a dashboard shell or leave pictures as placeholders.
- Full numerical defaults/thresholds in dossier remain proposed defaults, not newly user-ratified rules merely because summarized here. Each subproject brief captures its distinct model and evidence.
