# Applied Mathematics Project Series — Foundation

**Status:** Accepted series direction; individual builds are not asserted.

## Members

1. Road Trip Planner
2. Food Truck / Business Launch
3. Theme Park Designer
4. Mars Colony
5. Powers of Ten / Scale of Reality

Each project must connect a learning objective to a student action, representation, and observable evidence. Invalid mathematics should be blocked or corrected where appropriate; mathematically valid but strategically weak designs should receive consequence/feedback rather than being mislabeled wrong. Preserve each project's own duration, representations, context, and visual requirements.
